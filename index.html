FAQ

What time should I arrive?

Please arrive by 1:00 PM to allow time to be seated before the ceremony begins at 2:00 PM.

Can I bring a plus one?

Due to venue capacity, we are only able to accommodate those named on the invitation. Please refer to your invite for details.

Are children welcome?

Whilst we love your little ones, we have chosen to make our wedding an adults-only celebration. We hope this gives you a chance to enjoy the evening.

How do I get there?

Hartham Park is located in Corsham, Wiltshire. There is parking available on site. The nearest train station is Chippenham, approximately 10 minutes by taxi.
